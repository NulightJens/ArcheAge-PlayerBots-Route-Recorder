# Recording useful routes

Give each walk a clear start and destination. Walk the route normally and add a
quick mark at forks, merchants, quest locations or obstacles. Stop and export when
finished. Keep separate recordings for alternative paths and different travel modes.

Photos are optional. Keep the game in front while recording photos and confirm
the first F9 screenshot appears in the configured folder. Original screenshots
remain on your computer. The exported photo position is approximate.

Share the route ZIP and, if recorded, its separate photo ZIP. Add brief notes for
blocked sections, difficult turns or special travel requirements. Do not include
account credentials, private chat or unrelated screenshots.
